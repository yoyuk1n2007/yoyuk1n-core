Two Steps Forward Shirt

Historical Context
Part of Lang's exploration of semantic fashion. Using cryptic phrases as graphics, he challenged the viewer to interpret meaning within a commercial product. "Two Steps Forward" serves as both a literal movement and a philosophical stance on progress.
